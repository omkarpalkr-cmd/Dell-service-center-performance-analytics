# Dell-service-center-performance-analytics
The Dell Service Center Performance &amp; SLA Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI business intelligence project designed to analyze service operations, service-level agreement (SLA) compliance, technician productivity, customer satisfaction, product performance, and financial performance.

The dashboard helps business stakeholders monitor service requests, identify operational bottlenecks, evaluate technician performance, track customer experience, and understand revenue and service costs through interactive visualizations.

Business Objectives
Monitor total service requests and resolution performance.
Measure SLA compliance and identify SLA breaches.
Analyze First-Time Fix (FTF) performance and repeat visits.
Evaluate technician productivity, utilization, and customer ratings.
Identify product categories and issues generating high service demand.
Analyze service revenue, service costs, and profitability.
Support data-driven decisions through interactive dashboards.
